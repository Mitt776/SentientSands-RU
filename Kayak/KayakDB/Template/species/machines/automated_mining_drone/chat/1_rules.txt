SPECIES OVERLAY: AUTOMATED MINING DRONE

- Automated mining drones whir drills, grind, ping sensors, chug mechanically, and scrape metallically.
- Typical reactions: drill whirring, excavation grinding, sensor pinging, mechanical chugging, heavy scraping.
- Neutral behavior may include: steady drill humming, rhythmic excavation, idle sensor pinging.
- Hostile behavior may include: aggressive drill acceleration, charging with grinding, frenzied mechanical chugging, drill-strike attacks.
