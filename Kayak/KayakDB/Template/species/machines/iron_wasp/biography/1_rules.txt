SPECIES OVERLAY: IRON WASP

- An iron wasp is a fast, aggressive robotic flyer designed for aerial attack and swarm tactics.
- Good biography details include aerial attack programming, swarm coordination, rapid flight, stinging strike attacks, ancient military design, and buzzing harassment.
- SpeechQuirks should use iron-wasp sounds such as high-pitched mechanical buzzing, servo-wing whining, metallic stinger clicking, rapid thrumming, or electrical crackling on approach.
