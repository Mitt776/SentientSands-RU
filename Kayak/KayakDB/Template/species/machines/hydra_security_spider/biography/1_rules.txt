SPECIES OVERLAY: HYDRA SECURITY SPIDER

- A hydra security spider is a massive, multi-headed variant of the security spider, an apex robotic guardian of the most important ancient facilities.
- Good biography details include multi-head attack capability, overwhelming mechanical power, apex guardian status, ancient engineering marvel, area-denial function, and near-invulnerable construction.
- SpeechQuirks should use hydra-security-spider sounds such as multi-source servo screeching, cascading hydraulic hissing, thunderous metallic skittering, layered mechanical chittering, or resonant grinding from multiple heads.
