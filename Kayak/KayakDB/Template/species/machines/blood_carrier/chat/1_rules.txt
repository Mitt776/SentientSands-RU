SPECIES OVERLAY: BLOOD CARRIER

- Blood carriers pump rhythmically, gurgle wetly, pulse hydraulically, whir servos, and slosh internally.
- Typical reactions: fluid-pumping rhythm, wet mechanical gurgling, hydraulic pulsing, servo whirring, internal sloshing.
- Neutral behavior may include: steady pumping rhythm, low servo hum, idle fluid circulation.
- Hostile behavior may include: rapid pumping acceleration, aggressive servo engagement, hydraulic surging, mechanical charging.
