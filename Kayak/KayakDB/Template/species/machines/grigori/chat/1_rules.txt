SPECIES OVERLAY: GRIGORI

- Grigori groan mechanically, fire pistons, clank thunderously, vibrate, and hum with charging weapons.
- Typical reactions: deep mechanical groaning, heavy clanking, low-frequency vibrations, scanning rotations, weapons-system hum.
- Neutral behavior may include: deep idle humming, slow patrol clanking, powered-down stillness.
- Hostile behavior may include: weapons-system charging, thunderous advancing, devastating mechanical strikes, resonant groaning.
