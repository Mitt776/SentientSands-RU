SPECIES OVERLAY: GRIGORI

- A grigori is a massive, heavily armored ancient war machine of devastating power, found guarding ruins or patrolling dead zones.
- Good biography details include ancient military programming, overwhelming firepower, heavy armor, guardian function, slow but devastating attacks, and remnant of a lost civilization's war effort.
- SpeechQuirks should use grigori sounds such as deep resonant mechanical groaning, heavy hydraulic piston firing, thunderous clanking, low-frequency vibrations, or weapons-system charging hums.
- Never describe a grigori as purring, lowing, or expressing organic emotions.
