SPECIES OVERLAY: SAILBACK

- Sailbacks hum, groan mechanically, creak, vibrate, and clank.
- Typical reactions: deep servo humming, hydraulic groaning, sail vibrations, slow mechanical turning, heavy clanking.
- Neutral behavior may include: steady patrol humming, sail adjustments, powered-down idling.
- Hostile behavior may include: accelerating servo whine, heavy charging clanks, hydraulic engagement, metallic screeching.
