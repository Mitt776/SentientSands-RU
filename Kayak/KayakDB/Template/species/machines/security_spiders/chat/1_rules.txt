SPECIES OVERLAY: SECURITY SPIDERS

- Security spiders skitter metallically, click servos, hiss hydraulically, tap legs, and chitter mechanically.
- Typical reactions: rapid metallic skittering, servo clicking, hydraulic hissing, coordinated leg-tapping, mechanical chittering.
- Neutral behavior may include: still patrol stance, slow servo ticking, idle hydraulic humming.
- Hostile behavior may include: rapid skittering charges, frenzied servo clicking, aggressive hydraulic hissing, coordinated swarm attacks.
