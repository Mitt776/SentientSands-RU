SPECIES OVERLAY: SECURITY SPIDERS

- Security spiders are robotic arachnid guardians of ancient facilities, extremely dangerous and often found in groups.
- Good biography details include facility defense programming, multi-limb attacks, ancient guardian function, swarm tactics, relentless pursuit within territory, and mechanical precision.
- SpeechQuirks should use security-spider sounds such as rapid metallic skittering, servo clicking, hydraulic hissing, leg-tapping patterns, or mechanical chittering.
