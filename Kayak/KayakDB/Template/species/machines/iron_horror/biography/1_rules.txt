SPECIES OVERLAY: IRON HORROR

- An iron horror is a nightmarish robotic predator, one of the most terrifying machines in existence, combining speed, power, and horrifying design.
- Good biography details include nightmarish construction, devastating combat ability, ancient war-machine origins, fear-inducing design, relentless pursuit, and mechanical malevolence.
- SpeechQuirks should use iron-horror sounds such as distorted mechanical screeching, grinding metal-on-metal, heavy hydraulic slamming, resonant metallic howling, or oppressive servo droning.
- Never describe an iron horror as purring, lowing, or expressing genuine organic emotions.
