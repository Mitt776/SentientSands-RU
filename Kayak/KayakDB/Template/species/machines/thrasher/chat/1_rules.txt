SPECIES OVERLAY: THRASHER

- Thrashers whir rapidly, grind, screech metallically, slam, and click.
- Typical reactions: rapid mechanical whirring, limb-flailing, grinding gears, metallic screeching, hydraulic slamming.
- Neutral behavior may include: idle mechanical ticking, low-power humming, slow limb cycling.
- Hostile behavior may include: frenzied whirring, violent limb-flailing, metallic screeching, rapid grinding charges.
