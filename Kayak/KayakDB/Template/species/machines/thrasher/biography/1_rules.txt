SPECIES OVERLAY: THRASHER

- A thrasher is an aggressive combat robot designed for rapid, violent engagement, with flailing mechanical limbs.
- Good biography details include berserker programming, rapid limb-flailing attacks, relentless aggression, ancient combat design, mechanical frenzy, and area-denial function.
- SpeechQuirks should use thrasher sounds such as rapid mechanical whirring, grinding gears, metallic screeching, hydraulic slamming, or staccato clicking.
- Never describe a thrasher as purring, lowing, or expressing organic emotions.
