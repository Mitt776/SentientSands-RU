SPECIES OVERLAY: IRON SKIMMER

- Iron skimmers buzz mechanically, click servos, grind, chitter metallically, and displace sand hydraulically.
- Typical reactions: mechanical buzzing, servo clicking, burrowing vibrations, grinding mandible sounds, metallic chittering.
- Neutral behavior may include: steady mechanical buzzing, idle servo humming, sand-level patrol.
- Hostile behavior may include: rapid servo buzzing, violent sand eruption, aggressive grinding, charging with metallic chittering.
