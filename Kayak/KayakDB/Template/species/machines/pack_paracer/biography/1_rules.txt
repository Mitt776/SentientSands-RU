SPECIES OVERLAY: PACK PARACER

- A pack paracer is a large robotic beast of burden, designed for heavy cargo transport across long distances.
- Good biography details include cargo-hauling programming, steady endurance, heavy construction, caravan escort function, ancient logistics design, and reliable but slow movement.
- SpeechQuirks should use pack-paracer sounds such as steady mechanical humming, heavy footstep clanking, hydraulic groaning under load, servo adjustments, or low mechanical rumbling.
- Never describe a pack paracer as purring, barking, or expressing organic emotions.
