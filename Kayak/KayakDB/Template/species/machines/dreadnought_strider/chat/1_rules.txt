SPECIES OVERLAY: DREADNOUGHT STRIDER

- Dreadnought striders boom, groan, whine with charging weapons, creak, and clank.
- Typical reactions: booming footsteps, high mechanical groaning, weapons-charging whine, structural creaking, metallic booming.
- Neutral behavior may include: slow booming patrol steps, idle mechanical humming, structural settling.
- Hostile behavior may include: accelerating boom-steps, weapons-fire, devastating mechanical sweeps, resonant metallic roaring.
