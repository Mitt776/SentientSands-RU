SPECIES OVERLAY: SCREAMER

- Screamers scream, shriek, wail, howl, rasp, and screech.
- Typical reactions: piercing screaming, shrill shrieking, wailing, disorienting howls, rasping between screams.
- Friendly or trusting behavior is essentially nonexistent; screamers are feral predators.
- Agitated behavior may include: deafening screaming, frenzied shrieking, mass wailing, charging with howls, relentless screeching.
