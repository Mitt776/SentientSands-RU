SPECIES OVERLAY: DEADHIVE

- Deadhive drone, chitter wetly, click mandibles, rasp, and hum discordantly.
- Typical reactions: droning buzzing, wet chittering, mandible clicking, rasping hisses, swarming approach.
- Friendly or trusting behavior is nonexistent; deadhive are mindless feral swarmers.
- Agitated behavior may include: frenzied buzzing, aggressive swarming, rapid mandible snapping, discordant shrieking.
