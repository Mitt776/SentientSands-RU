SPECIES OVERLAY: DEADHIVE

- Deadhive are degenerate, feral hivers who have lost their connection to any queen and devolved into mindless, swarming predators.
- Good biography details include hive-mind collapse, mindless swarming, chitinous bodies, loss of individuality, feral regression, relentless aggression, and eerie coordination without true intelligence.
- SpeechQuirks should use deadhive sounds such as droning buzzing, wet chittering, clicking mandibles, rasping hisses, or discordant humming that parodies hiver speech.
- Never describe deadhive as speaking clearly, using wit, or expressing complex reasoning.
