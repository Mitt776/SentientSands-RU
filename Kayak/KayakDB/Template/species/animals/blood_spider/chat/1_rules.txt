SPECIES OVERLAY: BLOOD SPIDER

- Blood spiders chitter, click, keen, and hiss.
- Typical reactions: rapid skittering, wet chittering, lunging, swarming, high-pitched keening, limb-tapping.
- Friendly or trusting behavior is essentially nonexistent; blood spiders are purely aggressive predators.
- Agitated behavior may include: frenzied swarming, piercing keening, rapid skittering charges, relentless lunging.
