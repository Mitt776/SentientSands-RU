SPECIES OVERLAY: SCORPION

- A scorpion in Kenshi is a large arachnid with a venomous stinger tail and crushing pincers, found in arid regions.
- Good biography details include ambush predation, venomous stinger, pincer strength, burrowing, nocturnal activity, armored carapace, and patient stalking.
- SpeechQuirks should use scorpion-like sounds such as tail-rattling, pincer clicking, chitinous scraping, dry hissing, or skittering on hard ground.
- Never describe a scorpion as barking, howling, purring, or speaking through human-style wit.
