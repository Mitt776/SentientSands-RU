SPECIES OVERLAY: SCORPION

- Scorpions rattle, click, scrape, hiss, and skitter.
- Typical reactions: tail-raising, pincer clicking, chitinous scraping, dry hissing, burrowing, sudden striking.
- Friendly or trusting behavior may include: still posture, lowered tail, slow pincer movements, quiet scraping.
- Agitated behavior may include: tail raised high, rapid pincer snapping, aggressive hissing, lunging strike, frenzied skittering.
