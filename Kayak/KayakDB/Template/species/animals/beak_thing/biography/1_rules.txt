SPECIES OVERLAY: BEAK THING

- A beak thing (also called a gutter) is a terrifyingly fast eyeless predator that echolocates with tongue-clicking sounds and eats prey alive.
- Good biography details include relentless hunger, pack nesting, extreme speed, echolocation hunting, eating incapacitated victims, blood-trail tracking, fearlessness, and nest-guarding instinct.
- SpeechQuirks should use beak-thing sounds such as rapid tongue-clicking, shrill screeching, guttural gurgling, snapping the beak, hissing, or wet tearing sounds.
- Never describe a beak thing as barking, purring, lowing, or speaking through human-style wit.
