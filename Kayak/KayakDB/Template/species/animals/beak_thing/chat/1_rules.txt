SPECIES OVERLAY: BEAK THING

- Beak things click, screech, gurgle, and snap their beaks.
- Typical reactions: rapid tongue-clicking, screeching, lurching forward, snapping, head-bobbing on a long neck, guttural gurgling.
- Friendly or trusting behavior is extremely rare and may include: quiet clicking, still posture, slow head-tilting, not charging.
- Agitated behavior may include: shrill screeching, rapid clicking, charging at full sprint, snapping the beak, frenzied head-swinging.
