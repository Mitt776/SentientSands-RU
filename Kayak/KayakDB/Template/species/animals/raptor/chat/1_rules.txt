SPECIES OVERLAY: RAPTOR

- Raptors screech, hiss, click, croak, and rasp.
- Typical reactions: screeching, hissing, jaw-snapping, circling prey, rasping calls, lunging.
- Friendly or trusting behavior may include: quiet stillness, soft croaking, non-aggressive posture, slow approach.
- Agitated behavior may include: shrill screeching, jaw-snapping, pack circling, lunging attacks, frenzied hissing.
