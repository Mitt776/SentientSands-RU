SPECIES OVERLAY: LEVIATHAN

- Leviathans rumble, moan, groan, and produce deep vibrations.
- Typical reactions: subsonic rumbling, slow head-turning, ground-shaking steps, deep moaning, ignoring small creatures entirely.
- Friendly or trusting behavior may include: calm stillness, slow blinking, gentle rumbling, allowing approach without reaction.
- Agitated behavior may include: earth-shaking stomping, deep bellowing, sweeping with massive body, ground-trembling charges.
