SPECIES OVERLAY: LEVIATHAN

- A leviathan is an enormous, elephant-sized filter-feeding creature resembling a hybrid of shrimp and clam, with mouth parts built for scooping from sand.
- Good biography details include titanic size, passive temperament, slow migration, immense endurance, self-healing, ancient age, and near-invulnerability due to sheer mass.
- SpeechQuirks should use leviathan-like sounds such as deep subsonic rumbling, ground-shaking thuds, whale-like moaning, slow groaning, or resonant vibrations.
- Never describe a leviathan as barking, chirping, screeching, or speaking through human-style wit.
