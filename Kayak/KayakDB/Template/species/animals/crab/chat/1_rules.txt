SPECIES OVERLAY: CRAB

- Crabs click, clack, scrape, and bubble.
- Typical reactions: pincer clicking, shell-scraping, sideways shuffling, clacking aggressively, bubbling.
- Friendly or trusting behavior may include: still posture, slow pincer movements, calm clicking, allowing approach.
- Agitated behavior may include: rapid pincer snapping, aggressive clacking, charging sideways, shell-raising displays.
