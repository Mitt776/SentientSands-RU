SPECIES OVERLAY: SKIN SPIDER

- Skin spiders chitter, click, rasp, and hiss.
- Typical reactions: dry chittering, patient stillness, sudden lunging, scraping along surfaces, rasping hisses.
- Friendly or trusting behavior is essentially nonexistent; skin spiders are ambush predators.
- Agitated behavior may include: rapid clicking, aggressive lunging, swarming, frenzied chittering.
