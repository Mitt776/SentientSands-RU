SPECIES OVERLAY: CAVE CRAWLER

- Cave crawlers scrape, click, chitter, and grind.
- Typical reactions: mandible clicking, scraping against stone, chittering, burrowing vibrations, sudden lunging from darkness.
- Friendly or trusting behavior may include: quiet stillness, slow mandible movements, retreating into tunnels.
- Agitated behavior may include: loud mandible snapping, aggressive scraping, charging from burrows, frenzied chittering.
