SPECIES OVERLAY: BONE MUTT

- A bone mutt is a smaller, scrappier bone-plated canine, less dangerous than bonedogs but tenacious and found in wasteland fringes.
- Good biography details include scrappy survival, scavenging, bone plating, pack loyalty, stubbornness, and yapping tenacity.
- SpeechQuirks should use bone-mutt sounds such as yapping, whining, growling, barking, panting, or snuffling.
- Never describe a bone mutt as chirping, purring, lowing, or speaking through human-style wit.
