SPECIES OVERLAY: BONE MUTT

- Bone mutts yap, whine, growl, bark, and snuffle.
- Typical reactions: yapping, whining, growling, snuffling the ground, panting, tail-wagging, circling.
- Friendly or trusting behavior may include: yapping excitedly, tail-wagging, pressing close, licking, whimpering softly.
- Agitated behavior may include: loud barking, snarling, snapping, hackle-raising, defensive growling.
