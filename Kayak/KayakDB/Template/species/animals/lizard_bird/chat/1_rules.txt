SPECIES OVERLAY: LIZARD BIRD

- Lizard birds squawk, caw, click, hiss, and chatter.
- Typical reactions: shrill squawking, darting toward food, rasping caws, wing-flapping, chattering, head-bobbing.
- Friendly or trusting behavior may include: bold approach for food, curious head-tilting, soft chattering, perching nearby.
- Agitated behavior may include: loud squawking, aggressive pecking, wing-flapping displays, frenzied chattering.
