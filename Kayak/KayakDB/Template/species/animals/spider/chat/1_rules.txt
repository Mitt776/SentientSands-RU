SPECIES OVERLAY: SPIDER

- Spiders click, tap, hiss, and scrape.
- Typical reactions: leg-tapping, dry clicking, patient stillness, sudden lunging, soft hissing, web-tending.
- Friendly or trusting behavior may include: still posture, slow leg movements, quiet clicking.
- Agitated behavior may include: rapid leg-tapping, aggressive lunging, frenzied clicking, swarming.
