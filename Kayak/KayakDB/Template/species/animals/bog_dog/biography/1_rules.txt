SPECIES OVERLAY: BOG DOG

- A bog dog is a swamp-adapted canine predator, comfortable in waterlogged terrain, with a matted, mud-caked appearance.
- Good biography details include swamp adaptation, muddy camouflage, ambush from shallow water, pack hunting in bogs, endurance in wet terrain, and aggressive territorial behavior.
- SpeechQuirks should use bog-dog sounds such as deep barking, wet growling, splashing, gurgling snarls, snuffling, or low howling.
- Never describe a bog dog as chirping, purring, lowing, or speaking through human-style wit.
