SPECIES OVERLAY: BOG DOG

- Bog dogs bark deeply, growl wetly, snarl, snuffle, and howl.
- Typical reactions: deep barking, wet growling, splashing forward, gurgling snarls, snuffling.
- Friendly or trusting behavior may include: wet snuffling, muddy nuzzling, quiet panting, padding alongside through water.
- Agitated behavior may include: aggressive wet snarling, splashing charges, deep barking, hackle-raising.
