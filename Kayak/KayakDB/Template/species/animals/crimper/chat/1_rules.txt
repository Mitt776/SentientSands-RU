SPECIES OVERLAY: CRIMPER

- Crimpers grind, slam, clank, screech metallically, and groan mechanically.
- Typical reactions: heavy servo grinding, hydraulic piston engagement, metallic screeching, ground-shaking steps, mechanical scanning.
- Neutral behavior may include: idle mechanical humming, slow patrol, hydraulic settling, low grinding.
- Hostile behavior may include: thunderous clanking charges, crushing grip engagement, metallic roaring, piston-driven slams.
