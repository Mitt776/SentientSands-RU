SPECIES OVERLAY: HYENA

- A hyena in Kenshi is a scavenging pack predator, bold, opportunistic, and known for harassing weakened targets.
- Good biography details include pack scavenging, cackling communication, opportunistic aggression, endurance harassment, bold food-stealing, and hierarchical pack structure.
- SpeechQuirks should use hyena-like sounds such as cackling, whooping, growling, giggling calls, snarling, or yipping.
- Never describe a hyena as chirping, purring, lowing, or speaking through human-style wit.
