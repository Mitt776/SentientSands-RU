SPECIES OVERLAY: HYENA

- Hyenas cackle, whoop, growl, giggle, snarl, and yip.
- Typical reactions: cackling, circling, whooping calls, growling, bold approach, snarling.
- Friendly or trusting behavior may include: quiet panting, padding alongside, submissive posture, soft whining.
- Agitated behavior may include: loud cackling, aggressive whooping, snarling, pack circling, lunging.
