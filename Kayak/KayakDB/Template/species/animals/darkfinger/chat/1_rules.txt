SPECIES OVERLAY: DARKFINGER

- Darkfingers click softly, rasp, scrape, and skitter.
- Typical reactions: near-silent approach, soft clicking, sudden lunging, rasping, limb-flexing.
- Friendly or trusting behavior is essentially nonexistent; darkfingers are ambush predators.
- Agitated behavior may include: rapid clicking, aggressive lunging, frenzied multi-limb strikes, wet rasping.
