SPECIES OVERLAY: CAGE BEAST

- Cage beasts grunt, snuffle, rumble, and squeal.
- Typical reactions: soft grunting, snuffling at the ground, contented rumbling, curious approach, heavy breathing.
- Friendly or trusting behavior may include: following travelers, gentle snuffling, pressing close, happy grunting, nuzzling.
- Agitated behavior may include: startled squealing, lumbering retreat, panicked grunting, clumsy charging.
