SPECIES OVERLAY: LANDBAT

- Landbats shriek, click, chitter, and hiss.
- Typical reactions: high-pitched shrieking, chittering, crawling forward, flaring membrane, hissing, echolocating clicks.
- Friendly or trusting behavior may include: quiet chittering, hanging still, soft clicking, gentle nuzzling with the snout.
- Agitated behavior may include: piercing shrieks, lunging, frenzied chittering, baring fangs, rapid crawling.
