SPECIES OVERLAY: BEAK BOT

- A beak bot is a robotic version of a beak thing, emerged from hibernation mode, patrolling ancient crater regions to consume organic matter for bio-fuel.
- Good biography details include ancient construction, predatory programming mimicking beak things, bio-fuel hunger, patrol routines, mechanical relentlessness, and hybrid organic-machine nature.
- SpeechQuirks should use beak-bot sounds such as mechanical clicking, servo-driven screeching, hydraulic snapping, buzzing, or distorted versions of beak thing vocalizations.
- Never describe a beak bot as purring, lowing, or expressing genuine organic emotions.
