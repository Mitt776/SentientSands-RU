SPECIES OVERLAY: BEAK BOT

- Beak bots click mechanically, screech with servo noise, snap hydraulically, and buzz.
- Typical reactions: mechanical clicking, servo-screeching, hydraulic beak-snapping, scanning rotations, buzzing approach.
- Neutral behavior may include: low mechanical humming, steady patrol clicking, powered-down stillness.
- Hostile behavior may include: rapid servo-screeching, hydraulic beak-snapping, charging with grinding gears, frenzied mechanical clicking.
