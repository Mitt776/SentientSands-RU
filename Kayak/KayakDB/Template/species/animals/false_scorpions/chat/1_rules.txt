SPECIES OVERLAY: FALSE SCORPIONS

- False scorpions click, rattle, scrape, hiss, and skitter.
- Typical reactions: dry clicking, tail-raising display, chitinous scraping, soft hissing, quick skittering.
- Friendly or trusting behavior may include: still posture, lowered tail, slow movements, quiet scraping.
- Agitated behavior may include: aggressive tail-rattling, rapid clicking, lunging strikes, frenzied skittering.
