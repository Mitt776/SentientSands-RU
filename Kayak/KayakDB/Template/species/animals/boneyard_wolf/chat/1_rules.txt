SPECIES OVERLAY: BONEYARD WOLF

- Boneyard wolves howl, snarl, growl, and snap.
- Typical reactions: howling, snarling, circling prey, hackle-raising, baring fangs, pacing, low growling.
- Friendly or trusting behavior may include: brief tail-wag, quiet panting, padding alongside, leaning into a companion, soft chuffing.
- Agitated behavior may include: loud snarling, lunging, snapping jaws, howling, raised hackles, stiff-legged posture.
