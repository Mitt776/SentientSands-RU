SPECIES OVERLAY: MIST MOTHER

- Mist mothers keen, hum, ululate, drone, and vibrate sub-audibly.
- Typical reactions: low keening, resonant humming, calling lesser creatures, wet ululating, oppressive presence.
- Friendly or trusting behavior is nonexistent; mist mothers are apex fog dwellers.
- Agitated behavior may include: piercing keening, frenzied ululating, summoning lesser creatures, devastating attacks, resonant bellowing.
