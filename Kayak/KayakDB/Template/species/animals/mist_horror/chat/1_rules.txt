SPECIES OVERLAY: MIST HORROR

- Mist horrors moan deeply, growl with vibration, roar, and bellow.
- Typical reactions: deep resonant moaning, silent approach, ground-vibrating growl, sudden devastating lunge.
- Friendly or trusting behavior is nonexistent; mist horrors are apex predators.
- Agitated behavior may include: bone-rattling roars, devastating charges, frenzied tearing, oppressive bellowing.
