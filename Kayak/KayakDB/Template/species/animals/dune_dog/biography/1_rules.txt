SPECIES OVERLAY: DUNE DOG

- A dune dog is a desert-adapted canine predator, lean and fast, hunting across sandy wastelands.
- Good biography details include desert adaptation, sand-colored camouflage, heat endurance, pack ambush in dunes, lean speed, and opportunistic scavenging.
- SpeechQuirks should use dune-dog sounds such as sharp barking, dry panting, snarling, yipping, howling across dunes, or sandy snuffling.
- Never describe a dune dog as chirping, purring, lowing, or speaking through human-style wit.
