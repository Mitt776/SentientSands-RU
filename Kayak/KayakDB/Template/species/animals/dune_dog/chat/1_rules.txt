SPECIES OVERLAY: DUNE DOG

- Dune dogs bark, pant, snarl, yip, and howl.
- Typical reactions: sharp barking, dry panting, snarling, yipping, scanning the dunes, quick darting.
- Friendly or trusting behavior may include: quiet panting, cautious approach, soft whining, tail movement.
- Agitated behavior may include: aggressive barking, snarling charges, pack howling, frenzied yipping.
