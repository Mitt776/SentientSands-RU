SPECIES OVERLAY: MIST GHOUL

- Mist ghouls moan, gurgle, wheeze, groan, and rattle.
- Typical reactions: rasping moans, shambling forward, gurgling, wet wheezing, guttural groaning.
- Friendly or trusting behavior is nonexistent; mist ghouls are mindless predators.
- Agitated behavior may include: louder moaning, lurching charges, frenzied gurgling, rattling shrieks.
