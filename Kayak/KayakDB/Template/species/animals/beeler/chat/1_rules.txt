SPECIES OVERLAY: BEELER

- Beelers grind, click, drone, scrape, and rumble.
- Typical reactions: mandible grinding, carapace-clicking, slow advance, droning, chitinous scraping.
- Friendly or trusting behavior may include: still posture, quiet droning, slow mandible movements, passive feeding.
- Agitated behavior may include: aggressive mandible grinding, shell-rattling, charging, loud droning.
