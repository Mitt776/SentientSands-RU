SPECIES OVERLAY: CLEANER

- Cleaners whir, click, buzz, and hiss hydraulically.
- Typical reactions: servo whirring, mechanical scanning, hydraulic activation, metallic clicking, rotating toward threats.
- Neutral behavior may include: steady patrol rhythm, low servo hum, mechanical indifference, routine scanning.
- Hostile behavior may include: rapid servo acceleration, aggressive mechanical clicking, charging, hydraulic hissing intensifying.
