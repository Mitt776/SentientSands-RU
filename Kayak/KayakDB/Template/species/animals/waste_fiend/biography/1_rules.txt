SPECIES OVERLAY: WASTE FIEND

- A waste fiend is a mutated predator found in toxic wastelands, adapted to poisonous environments and driven by relentless hunger.
- Good biography details include toxic adaptation, wasteland survival, aggressive hunting, mutation-warped body, relentless pursuit, and pack ambush tactics.
- SpeechQuirks should use waste-fiend sounds such as ragged snarling, wet gurgling, rasping breaths, guttural howling, or choking hisses.
- Never describe a waste fiend as purring, lowing, or speaking through human-style wit.
