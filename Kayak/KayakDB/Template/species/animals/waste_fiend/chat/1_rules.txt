SPECIES OVERLAY: WASTE FIEND

- Waste fiends snarl, gurgle, rasp, howl, and hiss.
- Typical reactions: ragged snarling, wet gurgling, rasping breaths, lurching forward, guttural howling.
- Friendly or trusting behavior is essentially nonexistent; waste fiends are feral predators.
- Agitated behavior may include: frenzied snarling, wet howling, lunging charges, choking hisses, pack swarming.
