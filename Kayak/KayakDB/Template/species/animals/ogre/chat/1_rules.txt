SPECIES OVERLAY: OGRE

- Ogres bellow, roar, grunt, snort, and pound the ground.
- Typical reactions: deep bellowing, ground-pounding, guttural roaring, heavy snorting, lumbering forward.
- Friendly or trusting behavior may include: quiet grunting, still posture, slow approach, low rumbling.
- Agitated behavior may include: thunderous roaring, ground-shaking charges, frenzied pounding, aggressive bellowing.
