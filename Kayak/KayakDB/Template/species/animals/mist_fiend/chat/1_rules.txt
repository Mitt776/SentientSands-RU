SPECIES OVERLAY: MIST FIEND

- Mist fiends moan, hiss, rasp, keen, and click wetly.
- Typical reactions: low moaning, breathy hissing, emerging from mist, muffled rasping, soft keening.
- Friendly or trusting behavior is essentially nonexistent; mist fiends are stalking predators.
- Agitated behavior may include: sudden lunging, frenzied rasping, wet shrieking, coordinated mist ambush.
