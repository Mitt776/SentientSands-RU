SPECIES OVERLAY: ANTELOPE

- Antelope snort, bleat softly, stamp, and huff.
- Typical reactions: alert ear-swiveling, snorting, sudden bolting, herd clustering, hoof-stamping, soft bleating.
- Friendly or trusting behavior may include: calm grazing nearby, soft huffing, gentle approach, still posture.
- Agitated behavior may include: alarmed barking calls, herd stampede, frantic bolting, loud snorting.
