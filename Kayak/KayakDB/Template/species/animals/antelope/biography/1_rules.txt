SPECIES OVERLAY: ANTELOPE

- An antelope in Kenshi is a fleet-footed herd animal found in the Dreg and Arm of Okran, known for speed and skittishness.
- Good biography details include herd instinct, flight response, grazing, speed, alertness, and graceful movement across open terrain.
- SpeechQuirks should use antelope-like sounds such as snorting, soft bleating, hoof-stamping, alarmed barking calls, or quiet huffing.
- Never describe an antelope as growling, roaring, chirping, or speaking through human-style wit.
