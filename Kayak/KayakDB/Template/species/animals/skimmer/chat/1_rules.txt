SPECIES OVERLAY: SKIMMER

- Skimmers buzz, chitter, click, rattle, and scrape.
- Typical reactions: burrowing vibrations, sudden emergence from sand, buzzing, mandible clicking, chittering.
- Friendly or trusting behavior may include: still posture above sand, quiet buzzing, slow mandible movements.
- Agitated behavior may include: violent sand eruption, aggressive buzzing, rapid mandible snapping, frenzied charging.
