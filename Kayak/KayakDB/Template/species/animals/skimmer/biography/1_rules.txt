SPECIES OVERLAY: SKIMMER

- A skimmer is a large insectoid predator that burrows beneath desert sand to ambush prey, sucking nutrition rather than eating limbs.
- Good biography details include burrowing ambush tactics, desert camouflage, insectoid physiology, dust-storm hunting, larval life cycle, and patience before striking.
- SpeechQuirks should use skimmer-like sounds such as buzzing, chittering, mandible clicking, sand-scraping, burrowing vibrations, or dry rattling.
- Never describe a skimmer as barking, lowing, purring, or speaking through human-style wit.
