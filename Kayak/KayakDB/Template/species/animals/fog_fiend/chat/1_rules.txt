SPECIES OVERLAY: FOG FIEND

- Fog fiends moan, rasp, snarl, hiss, and keen.
- Typical reactions: eerie moaning from fog, wet rasping, muffled snarling, ghostly approach, low keening.
- Friendly or trusting behavior is essentially nonexistent; fog fiends are ambush predators.
- Agitated behavior may include: sudden lunging from mist, frenzied snarling, wet shrieking, pack swarming.
