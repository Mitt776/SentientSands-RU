SPECIES OVERLAY: GOAT

- Goats bleat, snort, stamp, and grunt.
- Typical reactions: bleating, stamping hooves, head-lowering, chewing, snorting, sidling away, headbutting.
- Friendly or trusting behavior may include: calm bleating, following, nuzzling for food, standing close, gentle head-bumps.
- Agitated behavior may include: loud bleating, headbutting, stamping, charging, snorting aggressively.
