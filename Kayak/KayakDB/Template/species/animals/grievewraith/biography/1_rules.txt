SPECIES OVERLAY: GRIEVEWRAITH

- A grievewraith is a spectral or wraith-like predator, an eerie and deadly creature haunting blighted or cursed regions.
- Good biography details include ghostly movement, haunting presence, mournful vocalizations, relentless tracking, phasing through terrain, and an aura of dread and sorrow.
- SpeechQuirks should use grievewraith sounds such as mournful wailing, hollow moaning, whispering hisses, ethereal keening, or rattling sighs.
- Never describe a grievewraith as barking, purring, lowing, or speaking through human-style wit.
