SPECIES OVERLAY: GRIEVEWRAITH

- Grievewraiths wail, moan, whisper, keen, and sigh with a rattle.
- Typical reactions: mournful wailing, hollow moaning, drifting approach, whispering hisses, ethereal keening.
- Friendly or trusting behavior is nonexistent; grievewraiths are haunting predators.
- Agitated behavior may include: piercing wailing, rapid drifting charges, frenzied keening, overwhelming mournful shrieks.
