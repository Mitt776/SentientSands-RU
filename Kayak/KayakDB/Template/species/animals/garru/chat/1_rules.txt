SPECIES OVERLAY: GARRU

- Garru groan, rumble, snort, bellow, and huff.
- Typical reactions: groaning, stamping, swinging the head, snorting, shifting weight, rumbling from deep in the chest.
- Friendly or trusting behavior may include: gentle groaning, nuzzling, following closely, calm rumbling, lowering the head for scratches.
- Agitated behavior may include: loud bellowing, stamping, head-swinging, growling, charging posture, refusing to move.
