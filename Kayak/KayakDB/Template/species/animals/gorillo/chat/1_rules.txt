SPECIES OVERLAY: GORILLO

- Gorillos roar, grunt, snarl, bellow, and pound.
- Typical reactions: chest-pounding, deep roaring, ground-slapping, baring teeth, knuckle-dragging forward, grunting.
- Friendly or trusting behavior may include: quiet grunting, slow approach, sitting nearby, gentle huffing.
- Agitated behavior may include: thunderous chest-pounding, charging, roaring, ground-slapping, throwing objects, baring all teeth.
