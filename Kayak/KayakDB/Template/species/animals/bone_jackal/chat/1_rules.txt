SPECIES OVERLAY: BONE JACKAL

- Bone jackals yip, growl, snarl, pant, and bark.
- Typical reactions: sharp yipping, circling cautiously, low growling, darting in and out, panting, snarling.
- Friendly or trusting behavior may include: quiet panting, cautious approach, soft whining, tail tucking.
- Agitated behavior may include: loud yipping, aggressive snarling, pack darting attacks, high-pitched barking.
