SPECIES OVERLAY: BONEDOG

- Bonedogs bark, growl, whine, and yip.
- Typical reactions: growling, snarling, barking, snapping, hackle-raising, tail-wagging, panting, circling, pawing.
- Friendly or trusting behavior may include: tail-wagging, licking, pressing close, playful barking, rolling over, whimpering softly.
- Agitated behavior may include: snarling, snapping, hackle-raising, baring teeth, low growling, lunging.
