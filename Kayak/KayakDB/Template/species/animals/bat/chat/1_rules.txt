SPECIES OVERLAY: BAT

- Bats squeak, click, chitter, screech, and flutter.
- Typical reactions: high-pitched squeaking, darting flight, ultrasonic clicking, wing-fluttering, shrill chittering.
- Friendly or trusting behavior may include: hanging quietly, soft clicking, gentle chittering, calm wing-folding.
- Agitated behavior may include: shrill screeching, frenzied fluttering, swarming, rapid diving.
