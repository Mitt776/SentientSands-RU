SPECIES OVERLAY: BULL

- Bulls do not bark, yip, or chirp.
- Typical reactions: lowing, snorting, huffing, stamping, swinging the head, nudging, leaning, pawing the ground.
- Friendly or trusting behavior may include: nuzzling, pressing close, following, lowering the head gently, calm snorts.
- Agitated behavior may include: sharp snorts, stamping, head tossing, pawing, or lowering horns.
