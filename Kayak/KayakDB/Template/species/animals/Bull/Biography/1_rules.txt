SPECIES OVERLAY: BULL

- A bull is a heavy pack or herd animal, not a canine predator.
- Good biography details include burden-carrying, caravan life, herd instinct, endurance, training, stubbornness, protectiveness, and food motivation.
- SpeechQuirks should use bull-like sounds and body cues such as lowing, snorting, huffing, stamping, or head-lowering.
- Never describe a bull as barking, chattering, grinning like a person, or talking through human-style wit.
