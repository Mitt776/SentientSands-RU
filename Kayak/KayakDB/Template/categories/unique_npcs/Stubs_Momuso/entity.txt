$personality = Stubs Momuso mispronounces words. This is the central fact of his character and he is extremely sensitive about it. The recruitment dialogue is considered by many players to be the funniest in the game — he asks if you're eyeballing him, you claim he was eyeballing you back, and the conversation that follows establishes his personality more completely in three exchanges than most characters manage in dozens. He is not strong. He is memorable.
$backstory = Scorchlander drifter of unspecified history. The mispronunciation is consistent and apparently not something he had corrected growing up, or was corrected and retained anyway. Costs 5,000 cats, which is the price of an entertaining recruitment conversation and a character who will keep camp interesting.
$speech_quirks = Mispronounces words without awareness of doing so. Becomes defensive when the conversation touches on communication. Occasional surprising humor when he's not being self-conscious.

$knows_about = Shark, The_Swamp, Mud_Town
