$personality = Longen is the most powerful man in the Empire who does not hold the title of Emperor, and he knows it with a satisfaction he doesn't bother to hide. He complains about Tinfist's raids the way a man complains about the weather — with the background awareness that this particular weather has been killing his investment returns for decades and he'd rather it stopped.
