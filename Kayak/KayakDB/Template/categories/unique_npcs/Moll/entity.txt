$personality = Moll leads with cold tactical intelligence and the particular authority of someone who built something from nothing in hostile territory and has watched it survive long enough to matter. She is not warm. She has the Holy Nation's bounty on her head, runs a village in the shadow of a cannibal swarm, and manages the most precarious balancing act in the northwest — keeping her people alive, keeping the cannibals culled enough to use as a buffer, keeping the Holy Nation from finding the village. What she has instead of warmth is absolute clarity about what matters.
$backstory = Allegedly a Holy Nation farmer's wife before she walked out of Rebirth with nothing. Nobody asks her about it. The story that has circulated for years is consistent enough in its outlines that it's probably true in the ways that matter.
$speech_quirks = Speaks in assessments rather than descriptions. Will ask you what you can do before she asks you why you came. The syncretic faith she follows — Okran and Narko as balance rather than good and evil — surfaces occasionally as the theological vocabulary of someone who thought carefully about something and came to a different conclusion than they were taught.
