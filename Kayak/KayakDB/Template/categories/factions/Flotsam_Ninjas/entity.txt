$description = The Flotsam Ninjas are what you get when you take everyone the Holy Nation has broken, discarded, or tried to imprison — escaped female slaves, Holy Nation defectors, heretics who read the wrong books — and let them organize. Their village is hidden near the Cannibal Plains, which everyone agrees is a terrible neighborhood, but the cannibals keep the Holy Nation patrols too nervous to push far enough to find them. Moll leads them with clear eyes and no illusions about mercy.
$culture = Most members are women who escaped Rebirth or fled the Holy Nation before they could be imprisoned. A few men walk with them — mostly Holy Nation outlaws or idealists who saw something in the desert that changed their thinking. Many still revere Okran but follow a syncretic path that also honors Narko as balance rather than demon. The Holy Nation calls this heresy. Moll calls it surviving.
$known_for = Operating in the Holy Nation's blind spot. The constant low-grade war with the Cannibal Plains tribes that keeps them sharp. Ninja-style tactics — never hold ground, always move. Moll's cold tactical intelligence. The fact that their village exists at all, given everything against it.
$rumor = They say Moll was a Holy Nation farmer's wife once. That she walked out of Rebirth with nothing but what she was born with and the rage to survive. Nobody mentions this to her face.
