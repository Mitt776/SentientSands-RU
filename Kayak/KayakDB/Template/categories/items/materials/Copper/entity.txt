$description = Raw copper ore, mined from copper deposits found near Admag, Squin, and several other locations. Processes into Copper Alloy Plates at an alloy smelter. Copper deposits have varying quality ratings — the ones near Admag run at around 10%, while the Hook Waystation's mines run at 80%. Worth knowing before committing to a mining operation. The difference between low and high quality copper deposits is one of the practical reasons settlement location matters beyond aesthetics.
