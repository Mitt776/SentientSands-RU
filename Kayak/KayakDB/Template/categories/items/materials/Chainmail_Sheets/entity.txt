$description = Chainmail Sheets are the most expensive standard crafting material in Kenshi — interlocked iron rings assembled into flat sheets at a Chain Armour Crafting Bench. The price reflects the labor: chainmail is time-consuming to assemble correctly, and the sheets are the intermediate product between that labor and the finished armor. Required for all chain armor pieces. Settlements with high-level armor smiths can produce them competitively; most players buy them from suppliers until they have the production infrastructure to make their own worthwhile.
