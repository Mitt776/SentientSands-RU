$description = A gi is a light cloth training garment — no armor protection, but it provides a bonus to the Martial Arts skill that makes it the base garment of choice for unarmed combat specialists. The Ninja Gi upgrades this concept with stealth bonuses; the Monk Robe takes it in a spiritual direction. The plain Gi is the utilitarian version: you wear it because you fight with your hands and you want the bonus, not because you care about the aesthetics. Available from clothing merchants.
