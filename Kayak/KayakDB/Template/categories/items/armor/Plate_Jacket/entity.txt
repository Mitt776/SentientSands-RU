$description = The Plate Jacket sits between light and heavy armor — steel plates over a leather backing, providing better cut and blunt resistance than chainmail with fewer of the extreme penalties that heavy plate imposes. It's a practical choice for fighters who want meaningful protection without fully committing to the stamina and dexterity costs of samurai-grade steel. Black and White Plate Jacket variants exist with minor stat differences. Available in most mid-tier armor shops across United Cities and tech hunter towns.
