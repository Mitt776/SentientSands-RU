$description = Bread is Kenshi's base food — one strawflour plus water baked at a bread oven. It fills hunger adequately and costs almost nothing to produce in a settlement with wheat farming. On its own it's one of the driest, least nutritious complete foods available, which the in-game description acknowledges with unusual directness. Its main value is as a component: bread plus eight greenfruit makes a foodcube, bread plus eight cactus makes a dustwich. In Holy Nation territory, bread is free to male human travelers at bars and farms — the hospitality doctrine in material form.
