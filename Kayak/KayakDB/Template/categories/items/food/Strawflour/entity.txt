$description = Strawflour is milled wheatstraw — the intermediate product between raw grain and bread. Ten wheatstraw units produce one strawflour at a grain silo, and strawflour combined with water produces bread at a bread oven. The processing chain is: wheatstraw → strawflour → bread → foodcubes or dustwiches. It's the link in the food production chain that most players don't stock properly at first, creating grain silos full of wheatstraw and bread ovens sitting idle.
