$description = The Deadcat Fishing Villages are two small settlements — one on the Northern Coast north of World's End, one walled village east of Black Scratch in the Outlands. Both belong to the Deadcat faction. The Northern Coast one has a bar, a general trader, and a fish salesman; the Outlands one is more minimal with a bar and fish shops. They're genuinely peaceful stops in areas that aren't otherwise well-served by commerce. The Deadcat faction is itself small and quiet — fishermen who managed to avoid the political conflicts that define most of Kenshi by being in places nobody particularly wanted to fight over.
