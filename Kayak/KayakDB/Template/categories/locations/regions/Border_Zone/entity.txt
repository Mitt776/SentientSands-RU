$description = The Border Zone is the contested space between the Holy Nation's western territories and the Shek Kingdom's eastern edges, threaded through with trade roads that nobody can quite control and everybody needs. Dust Bandits and Starving Bandits contest the roads. Black Dragon Ninjas operate in the shadows. A Waystation provides some shelter. It is the first thing most travelers see after leaving the relative safety of the starting region, and it teaches them quickly.
