$description = The Crater is exactly what the name says — a large impact or blast formation in the southern territories. Whether it was caused by something falling from orbit, a weapon deployed during the War of Behemoths, or a geological event is not agreed upon. What is agreed upon is that the terrain at the center is strange, the mineral composition is unusual, and the zone functions as a natural boundary that most factions treat as a limit of practical operations.
